Preserve government application search, platform links, original images and native administration. Decode keyed link objects while keeping legacy extra links.

Include multi-platform filtering, expandable descriptions and icon fallback, plus capability-gated multilingual metadata, activation/order, icon upload and confirmed deletion. The source public page shows icons rather than a screenshot gallery.
