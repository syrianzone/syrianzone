# ios: bring the full homepage catalogue and widgets to iPhone

Replace the two-section landing screen with the website catalogue, native search, original section artwork, light/dark settings, configurable links and widgets. Synchronize supported account preferences while preserving device choices for website-only themes.

This group also owns the shared navigation shell, canonical route aliases, Arabic/RTL native controls, focus-aware public loaders, map/layout adapters and global versioned campaign banner. Keep Saved and Settings reachable, suppress campaigns across the transit family and retain dismissal by campaign version. Sister applications and external resources remain deliberate outbound links.

Validation: Native homepage tests have exercised search, theme changes, toolbar bounds and opening Games. Native40 passed the corrected Home case in 27.852 seconds, including a measured near-edge Settings press inside its host but outside the smaller button accessibility frame, followed by the existing theme/search checks. The product icon style stayed unchanged. Native48 repeated the current full-product Home toolbar, measured host-edge Settings callback, light/dark and exact-search checks successfully in 28.638 seconds. Native48 overall had three passes, one failure and zero skips or runtime warnings; the separate full system Reduce Motion case failed. Native40 remains a recorded eight-pass, two-failure, zero-skip cohort. Account-native07 passed campaign-overlay geometry, visit-only quieting without moving Home content and reappearance on relaunch. The separate local administrator case passed campaign-title save, reopen and restoration. Native11 later passed the fixture-only long-popup case in 17.840565 seconds: the full body label remained in accessibility, all three controls were initially contained/hittable, and primary/Later shared one row. It did not open the link, save server settings or persist dismissal. Maximum 500-character/DynamicType layout and the remaining integrated interactions are separate checks.

Startup observations: Three cold launches of the current deferred variant reached a responsive first frame in 2.8063, 2.8043 and 2.8551 seconds (mean 2.8219). The matching eager variant averaged 2.9251 seconds across three earlier samples. Both used one simulator, with eager measured first. This small ordered comparison does not establish a significant gain, memory improvement or faster physical-device startup. First-open tab behavior still needs its native result; the earlier eager tab case was blocked by a campaign overlay.

Dependencies: Shared navigation, storage and account changes.
